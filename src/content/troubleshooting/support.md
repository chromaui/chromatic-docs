# Support

The fastest way to get help is to search the docs and browse our [FAQ](/docs/faq).

Our team is spread across timezones for a quick turnaround time. Use our **in-app chat** or [email](mailto:support@chromatic.com) to get in touch. Please provide details like version numbers, logs, and screenshots, to speed up troubleshooting.

Go to our [Status page](https://status.chromatic.com/) to check uptime and get updates on incidents.
