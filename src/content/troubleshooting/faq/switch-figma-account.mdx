# How do I reconnect or switch my Figma account?

To reconnect or switch your Figma account:

1. Open your [profile settings](https://www.chromatic.com/profile) and disconnect Figma.
2. Connect the Figma account you want to use.
3. Re-enable [Figma in Chromatic](/docs/figma-in-chromatic#enable) under **Manage » Configure » Connected applications** for each affected project.

Disconnecting Figma from your profile disables the integration for every project connected through that profile. Reconnecting your profile does not re-enable those projects automatically.

If the **Designs** tab is missing from your component pages, first check the project's Figma connection under **Manage » Configure » Connected applications**. If it uses a teammate's Figma account, ask that teammate to check their connection.
