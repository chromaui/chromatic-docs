# How do I reconnect or switch my Figma account?

To reconnect or switch your Figma account, open your [profile settings](https://www.chromatic.com/profile), disconnect Figma, then connect the account you want to use.

If the **Designs** tab is missing from your component pages, first check that [Figma in Chromatic](/docs/figma-in-chromatic#enable) is enabled under **Manage → Connected Applications** for the project. If it is enabled, reconnect Figma from your profile settings.
