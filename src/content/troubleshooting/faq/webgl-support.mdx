# Does Chromatic support WebGL?

**No.** Chromatic does not fully support WebGL rendering. If your component renders content to a WebGL canvas (for example, via PixiJS, Three.js, Babylon.js, or another GPU-backed renderer), you may see unstable or flaky visual snapshots that cannot be fully stabilized through configuration on the Chromatic side.

WebGL rendering is controlled entirely by the client-side library and the GPU. Font rasterization, layout, and compositing all happen asynchronously in the browser. As a result:

- Chromatic cannot force a deterministic frame for capture.

- Chromatic cannot inspect the internal contents of a WebGL canvas.

- What Chromatic captures is the final composited output of the browser's compositor — the WebGL canvas layer is composited into the image, with no inspectable DOM nodes involved.

Because of these constraints, there is no way for Chromatic to guarantee identical output between two captures of the same page.
