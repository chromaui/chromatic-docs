# Does Chromatic support WebGL?

No, Chromatic doesn't fully support WebGL rendering. If your component renders content to a WebGL canvas (for example, with PixiJS, Three.js, Babylon.js, or another GPU-backed renderer), you may see unstable or flaky visual snapshots that can't be fully stabilized through Chromatic configuration.

WebGL rendering is controlled entirely by the client-side library and the GPU. Font rasterization, layout, and compositing all happen asynchronously in the browser. As a result:

- Chromatic can't force a deterministic frame for capture.
- Chromatic can't inspect the internal contents of a WebGL canvas.
- Chromatic captures the final output of the browser's compositor. The WebGL canvas layer is composited into that image, with no inspectable DOM nodes involved.

Because of these constraints, Chromatic can't guarantee identical output between two captures of the same page.
