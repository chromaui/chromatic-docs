# How do Figma, Storybook, and Chromatic work together?

You can connect Figma and Storybook with these tools:

- [Storybook Connect](/docs/figma-plugin) shows stories beside designs in Figma.
  - [Figma in Chromatic](/docs/figma-in-chromatic) shows the linked designs beside stories in Chromatic.
- [Storybook's Designs addon](https://storybook.js.org/addons/@storybook/addon-designs) shows designs in Storybook.

You can use these together. Connections between stories and Figma components made through Storybook Connect or Figma in Chromatic appear in both Figma and Chromatic.
