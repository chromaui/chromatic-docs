# What's the difference between Figma in Chromatic and Figma Plugin?

There are two Figma integrations and a Storybook addon:

- [Figma in Chromatic](/docs/figma-in-chromatic) shows designs beside stories in Chromatic.
- [Storybook Connect](/docs/figma-plugin) shows stories beside designs in Figma.
- [Storybook's Designs addon](https://storybook.js.org/addons/@storybook/addon-designs) shows designs in Storybook.

You can use these together. Connections between stories and Figma components made through Storybook Connect or Figma in Chromatic appear in both integrations.
