# How do I delete my profile?

1. Open your [profile page](https://www.chromatic.com/profile).
2. Under **Danger zone**, select **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

If you signed up with an email and a password, Chromatic asks for that password before deleting.

If your profile signs in through [SSO](/docs/access/sso), the **Danger zone** section isn't shown and you can't delete the profile yourself. Use our **in-app chat** to contact us or email us at [support@chromatic.com](mailto:support@chromatic.com), and we'll remove it.

## What deletion removes

Deleting your profile permanently deletes the projects on your personal account. They can't be recovered.

Projects on organization accounts aren't deleted. Your profile loses access to them, and the projects stay with the account.

To deactivate or delete an organization account, use our **in-app chat** to contact us or email us at [support@chromatic.com](mailto:support@chromatic.com).

<div class="callout">

⚠️ **We usually can't move projects to another account for you.** If your personal account holds projects you still need, contact us via our **in-app chat** or email [support@chromatic.com](mailto:support@chromatic.com) before you delete anything.

</div>
