# How do I cancel a build?

Chromatic automatically cancels a build that is stuck in progress for 2 hours. If it remains in progress longer, use our **in-app chat** to contact us or email us at [support@chromatic.com](mailto:support@chromatic.com). Include a link to the build so we can investigate it.
