# How do I cancel a build?

Chromatic automatically marks a build as failed if it is still in progress more than 2 hours after it was created. If it remains in progress longer, use our **in-app chat** to contact us or email us at [support@chromatic.com](mailto:support@chromatic.com). Include a link to the build so we can investigate it.
