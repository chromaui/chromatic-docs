# Is VPN supported?

Using a VPN is supported. If your organization has IP restrictions enabled, add [Chromatic's IP addresses](/docs/faq/allowlist-ips-for-git-providers) to your allowed list.

If your firewall or proxy restricts connections from your CI environment, follow our [firewall allowlist guidance](/docs/faq/add-chromatic-to-allowlist-if-my-resources-are-behind-a-firewall).

Chromatic uses port 443 for these connections.
