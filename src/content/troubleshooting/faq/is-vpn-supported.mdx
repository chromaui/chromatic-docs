# Is VPN supported?

Yes. Your CI environment can use a VPN when it connects to Chromatic.

If GitHub Enterprise Server or self-managed GitLab is on a private network, [link it through a public HTTPS proxy](/docs/faq/link-a-repository#link-an-on-premise-provider) and allow [Chromatic's outbound IP addresses](/docs/faq/allowlist-ips-for-git-providers) through your firewall.

If your firewall or proxy restricts connections from your CI environment, follow our [firewall allowlist guidance](/docs/faq/add-chromatic-to-allowlist-if-my-resources-are-behind-a-firewall).

Your CI environment connects to Chromatic over port 443.
